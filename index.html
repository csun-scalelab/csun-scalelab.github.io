Smart Coordination and Locomotion Laboratory

We study how biological systems move, sense, and coordinate, and use those principles to design and improve engineered systems.

Research Focus

Bio-inspired Locomotion

Studying how biological organisms — from sea stars to fish — interact with the physical world to move efficiently, and translating those insights into robotic systems.

AI-Powered Swimmers

Using Reinforcement Learning to discover optimal locomotion strategies and sensory feedback policies for bio-inspired underwater robots.

Decentralized Multi-Agent Control

Building mathematical models of collective behavior in fish schools and other biological groups to design scalable, distributed robotic systems.
